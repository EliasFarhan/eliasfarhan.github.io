As most of you already know, the Soup Raiders game was in developer limbo for a while. With everyone in the team doing their thing, the project was not moving forward. There was a talk at Reboot in 2023 where the speakers talked about release your game even when it's not complete and with the current situation on Soup Raiders, I decided to release the latest demo <a href="https://teamkwakwa.itch.io/soup-raiders" target="_blank">here</a>. This vertical slice contains one island with the Docks, the Philosofish Palace and the Boss battle, with the Seagulls tutorials and a fight with the two big guard dogs. 

A huge thanks to Julie Baechtold and Camille Bovey on the art team, as well as David Wise for the music! Little mention to Guillaume Laugé who helped me with the battle UI as well. Also, thank you to Pro Helvetia and the Canton de Vaud for their fundings that allow us to bring this demo to live. 

Soup Raiders is not over, but work on this project is coming to a hold. I am currently working on Beach Slap, you can wishlist it <a href="https://store.steampowered.com/app/3170110/Beach_Slap/" target="_blank">here</a>.
